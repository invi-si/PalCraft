# Original NPC skin artwork

Both designs were generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool, then imported into the game's exact 64x64 wide-arm UV layout by `tools/release/pack_npc_skins.ps1`. The import step samples the generated face artwork and packs the standard base-model rectangles; unused/overlay texels stay transparent. Original generated atlases are retained in `art/source/` in the public tree. No existing Mojang skin was used as an edit target or reference.

Bram prompt: An original bearded village toolsmith. Grey short-sleeve shirt, brown leather apron tied over the shirt with a tool belt, dark charcoal trousers, brown work boots, warm medium skin, short dark brown hair and a clear dark beard. Both arms are normal human arms with matching grey sleeves and bare forearms. No mechanical arm, no overalls, no red shirt, no logos or lettering. Visually distinct from every Mojang default character. Flat transparent square texture atlas, classic 64x64 wide-arm player-skin UV layout, crisp pixel art; every base-model face opaque and all second-layer regions transparent. No portrait, 3D preview, captions or shadows outside UV rectangles.

Rina prompt: An original village fletcher and farmer. Forest-green tunic with a diagonal brown leather quiver strap across torso/back, brown trousers and brown boots, warm tan skin, dark brown braided hair, friendly brown eyes. No red shirt, white blouse, hat, logos or lettering. Both arms normal; clearly distinct from Mojang default characters. Flat transparent 64x64 wide-arm UV texture atlas, crisp pixel blocks, no 3D preview. Standard head/body/arm/leg base faces opaque, all unused/overlay regions transparent.

Final game files are `Assets/bram.png` and `Assets/rina.png` in the Islands & Animals payload. English/Korean names and roles live in `npc_text.lua`; old trade journal limits migrate through `npc_legacy.lua`. Alex uses `assets/minecraft/textures/entity/player/wide/alex.png`, extracted locally from the user's own jar.
